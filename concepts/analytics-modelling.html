Data grain

State what one row represents.

Examples include one expense, one approval cycle, or one organisation-month.

Star schema

Separate events from context.

Facts hold measures. Dimensions provide time, organisation, vendor, and category context.

Data marts

Serve one decision area.

A mart gives users a stable view for spend, contracts, approvals, or service performance.

Semantic layer

Define the metric once.

The dashboard reads governed measures instead of rebuilding business logic.
