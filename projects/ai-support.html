My approach

Build a controlled evaluation sample

I reduced 28,587 source tickets to 16,338 English records and evaluated 300 successful classifications.

Compare predictions with ground truth

I measured accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and confusion matrices instead of trusting model confidence.

Set the automation boundary from evidence

I retained human review for priority and department routing and qualified only a limited category case.

Business impact

Prevented weak predictions from becoming automated decisions.

Governance decision

Do not automate an unvalidated decision.

Model confidence does not replace measured accuracy.

Decision evidence

Where can AI assist safely?

The dashboard separates observed operations, measured model performance, and recommendation evidence.

01 · Operating baseline

Is the opportunity material?

Why this visual exists. Ticket volume is kept separate from a scenario model for possible time and cost savings.

Finding

The dataset has 16,338 English tickets. A 50% coverage scenario estimates 544.6 hours and £9,802.80 saved.

Interpretation

The workload is observed. The savings are simulated from explicit time and cost assumptions.

Decision

Use the scenario to size interest, not to justify automation before validation.

02 · Validation and routing

Which decisions pass the evidence gate?

Why this visual exists. Accuracy, confusion, coverage, and estimated error risk are shown beside the routing policy.

Finding

Category accuracy is 63.3%. Priority is 38.0%, and department is 27.7%. Only 89 Request cases reach 100% observed category accuracy.

Interpretation

Model confidence is not a control. Empirical performance limits automation to one narrow category case.

Decision

Allow limited Request category routing. Keep priority, department, and other categories under human review.

03 · Root cause

Where should the team reduce demand?

Why this visual exists. Recurring themes are linked to evidence-bounded self-service actions, while wording-quality issues remain visible.

Finding

Integration support is the largest named theme at 66 tickets, or 22.0% of the classified sample. Unmapped wording is 30.3%.

Interpretation

The theme supports a content opportunity, not a forecast of ticket deflection.

Decision

Improve integration setup and troubleshooting guidance, then measure adoption and ticket outcomes.

Implementation proof · Python

Convert measured accuracy into a routing rule.

The policy uses empirical accuracy and sample size. Predictions that fail either condition remain under human review.

recommendations = accuracy.copy()
eligible = (
    (recommendations["empirical_accuracy"] >= decision_threshold)
    & (recommendations["ticket_count"] >= minimum_sample_tickets)
)
recommendations["routing_recommendation"] = eligible.map(
    {True: "Auto-route category", False: "Human review"}
