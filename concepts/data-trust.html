Concepts / Data trust

Data systems

Trust must follow the data.

A useful number has a known source, a controlled path, a matched total, and a clear refresh time.

Visual model

Trace it. Test it. Reconcile it.

Each control answers a different trust question. Together, they make a reporting result explainable.

Data lineage

Where did it come from?

Keep the source file, record origin, and transformation path visible.

Reconciliation

Do totals still match?

Compare counts and values before and after each important transformation.

Freshness

Is the delivery current?

Separate the business date from the time when the pipeline received the data.

Control rule

Do not hide exceptions.

Keep rejected and review-required records visible outside the published total.
