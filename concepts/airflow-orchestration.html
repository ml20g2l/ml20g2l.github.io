Airflow orchestration

Airflow controls when tasks run, which task depends on another task, and what happens after failure.

Visual model

A workflow is more than a schedule.

The dependency graph prevents a downstream task from using incomplete upstream data.

Why it matters

Order protects the output.

A dashboard refresh must not start before validation and modelling finish.

Failure handling

Stop at the failed task.

The workflow records the failure and keeps downstream tasks from publishing incomplete data.

Safe recovery

Rerun the controlled path.

Idempotent loads support recovery without duplicate data.
